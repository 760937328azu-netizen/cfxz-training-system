Remove only the flat beige/brown outside background around the island and replace that outside area with one perfectly uniform solid chroma-magenta color (#ff00ff), including the outer corners and all empty space beyond the island silhouette. Do not add any text, labels, badges, people, icons, extra structures, borders, frames, or shadows outside the island. Keep the island edges clean and complete so the chroma background can be converted into a transparent alpha channel.
